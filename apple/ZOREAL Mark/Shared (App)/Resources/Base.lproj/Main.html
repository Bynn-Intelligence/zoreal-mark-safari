ZOREAL Mark

Shows you which posts a real human, verified by ZOREAL, stands behind, and lets you sign your own. This app carries the Safari extension; everything happens in Safari.

Turn the extension on in Settings > Apps > Safari > Extensions, then allow it on all websites so it can verify a Mark wherever one appears.
